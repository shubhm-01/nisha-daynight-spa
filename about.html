ABOUT NISHA DAYNIGHT SPA

A pause in the middle
of a busy day.

Nisha Daynight Spa is located on Jalna Road in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. Our intention is simple: make it easier to explore massage, skin and body-care options, understand what suits you and request a session with clarity.

01 / A CALMER PACE

Leave a little room
for stillness.

Every visit begins with understanding the kind of session you are looking for — a relaxing full-body massage, a focused treatment or a skin and body-care ritual. Ask questions, share your preferred pressure and confirm details before the session.

02 / THOUGHTFUL CHOICES

Choose what feels
right for you.

Browse the treatment menu and ask our team about the experience, duration, price and current availability. Specialist treatments are confirmed only when a suitably trained therapist and the required setup are available.

03 / YOUR OWN MOMENT

Come as you are.
Take your time.

Whether you have a short window or want to plan a longer visit, get in touch before arriving. We will help you understand the options and request a suitable appointment.
